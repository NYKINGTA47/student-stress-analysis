# Student Stress Analysis Dashboard

## Project Overview

This project analyzes student stress levels and their relationship with mental health and academic performance. Using Power BI, I created interactive dashboards to identify trends between stress, anxiety, depression, sleep quality, and academic achievement.

The goal of this project is to explore how stress impacts student well-being and academic outcomes through data visualization and exploratory data analysis.

---

## Dataset Source

* Platform: Kaggle
* Dataset: Student Stress Monitoring Datasets
* Author: Md Sultanul Islam Ovi
* Dataset Link: https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/mdsultanulislamovi/student-stress-monitoring-datasets

The dataset contains 843 student survey responses and includes psychological, academic, social, and lifestyle factors related to student stress and well-being.

---

## Research Question

How do stress levels affect anxiety, depression, sleep quality, and academic performance among students?

---

## Dashboard Insights

* Students with higher stress levels report higher anxiety levels.
* Students with higher stress levels report higher depression levels.
* Sleep quality decreases as stress levels increase.
* Academic performance decreases as stress levels increase.

---

## Summary of Results

* Higher stress levels were associated with increased anxiety.
* Depression levels increased as student stress levels increased.
* Sleep quality declined as stress levels increased.
* Academic performance decreased among students experiencing higher stress.
* Stress appeared to negatively affect both mental health and academic outcomes.
