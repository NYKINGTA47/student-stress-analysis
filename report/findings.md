# Student Stress Analysis Findings

## Research Question

How do sleep quality, study load, anxiety, peer pressure, and career concerns affect student stress levels?

## Dataset

The dataset contains psychological, academic, social, and environmental factors related to student stress.

## Variables Examined

- Stress Level
- Study Load
- Sleep Quality
- Anxiety Level
- Future Career Concerns
- Peer Pressure
- Social Support
- Academic Performance
- Bullying

## Planned Analysis

The analysis examines relationships between stress and key academic, psychological, and social factors.

## Future Work

Results will be visualized using Power BI dashboards.
